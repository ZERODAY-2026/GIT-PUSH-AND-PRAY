RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 1 April 2026 between Mr. A. Sharma (the "Landlord") and Ms. R. Rao (the "Tenant") for the flat at 12, Lake View Apartments, Dharwad.

1. TERM
The tenancy begins on 1 April 2026 and continues for eleven (11) months, ending on 28 February 2027, unless ended earlier under this agreement.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 15,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after the 5th.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 45,000. The Landlord shall refund the deposit within 30 days of the Tenant vacating, after deducting unpaid rent and any damages beyond normal wear and tear as the Landlord reasonably determines.

4. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Minor repairs costing under Rs. 1,000 are the Tenant's responsibility. Structural repairs are the Landlord's responsibility. The Tenant must not make alterations without written consent.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement by giving two (2) months' written notice. If the Tenant leaves without notice, the Landlord may forfeit up to two months' rent from the deposit. The Landlord may terminate immediately if rent is unpaid for two consecutive months.

6. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
The Landlord may enter the premises at reasonable times to inspect the property.

7. GENERAL
Any dispute shall be resolved by arbitration in Dharwad. This agreement may be modified only in writing signed by both parties.
